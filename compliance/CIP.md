## 1. Purpose and scope

Section 326 of the USA PATRIOT Act and 31 CFR 1023.220 require a broker-dealer to have a
written Customer Identification Program. Alpaca is the broker-dealer of record and
performs identity verification; the Company operates the customer-facing onboarding and
transmits the CIP data. This document describes the Company's obligations in that role.

A "customer" is a natural person who opens a brokerage account through the platform.
The Company does not currently onboard entities, trusts, or non-US persons _[decide:
confirm this restriction is enforced and stated in the customer agreement]_.

## 2. Information collected before an account is opened

The following are collected from the customer directly, in the onboarding flow, and
transmitted to Alpaca in the account application. None of it is defaulted, inferred, or
generated by the Company.

| Field | Purpose | Enforcement |
|---|---|---|
| Legal first and last name | CIP identity | rejected under 2 characters |
| Date of birth | CIP identity; 18+ eligibility | must parse; age 18–120 |
| Residential street address, city, state, ZIP | CIP address | all required; 2-letter state; 5-digit ZIP |
| Social Security number | CIP taxpayer identification | required; format and structural validity checked |
| Phone number | contact and verification | 10 digits, US |
| Country of citizenship | eligibility and tax treatment | defaults to USA; must be a 3-letter code |
| Source of funds | AML risk assessment | must be one of Alpaca's accepted values |
| Four regulatory disclosures | control person, broker-dealer affiliation, political exposure, immediate family exposure | must be explicitly answered; no default |
| Customer agreement acceptance | signed-agreement evidence | must be affirmatively accepted |

**Nothing is substituted.** The account-application builder raises an error rather than
supplying a placeholder for a missing field. This is deliberate: an application filed
with invented identity data is a false attestation regardless of intent, and the code
must make that impossible rather than merely unlikely.

**Disclosures must be answered, not defaulted.** The four questions are stored as
`null` until the customer selects an answer, so "not answered" is distinguishable from
"answered no," and the application is rejected in both the client and the server if any
remain unanswered.

## 3. Evidence of agreement

Each account application records, alongside the customer agreement:

- the timestamp at which the customer accepted it, captured client-side at acceptance;
- the IP address the acceptance was received from.

The IP is taken from the socket, and from `X-Forwarded-For` **only** when the
`TRUST_PROXY` environment variable is explicitly set — which is done solely where the
platform is known to sit behind a trusted, TLS-terminating proxy. Trusting a
caller-supplied header by default would let any client dictate the IP recorded on a
regulatory filing.

## 4. Verification

Identity verification (CIP/KYC) is performed by Alpaca through its verification
provider. The Company does not independently verify identity and does not represent to
customers that it does.

Application outcomes and the Company's handling:

| Outcome | Handling |

| Approved / active | account is usable; funding may proceed |
| Submitted / pending | account exists but is not yet fundable; the customer is shown a pending state |

## 5. Recordkeeping

Under 31 CFR 1023.220(a)(3), CIP records are retained for **five years after the
account is closed**.

The Company retains:

- the identifying information listed in §2, **excluding the Social Security number**;
- the last four digits of the SSN, for support and account matching;
- the customer agreement acceptance timestamp and IP;
- an append-only audit trail of account-relevant events (signup, account creation,
  email verification, deposits, withdrawals, residue routing, password resets).

**The full SSN is never written to Company storage.** It is transmitted to Alpaca in the
account application and then discarded. It has no ongoing operational use, and holding
it would make the Company a breach target for data it does not need. Alpaca retains the
CIP record as the broker-dealer.

## 8. Related controls

- Access to customer data is limited to the production environment and its operators.
- Secrets live in environment variables, never in source control.
- Session cookies are HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, and Secure in production.
- Cross-site state-changing requests are rejected before any handler runs.
- Passwords are scrypt-hashed with a per-user salt and compared in constant time.
- Rate limits apply to signup, login, password reset, and verification email.
